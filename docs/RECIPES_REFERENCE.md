### Stone Age

#### Wild Grass Gruel
- **Origin:** Global temperate grasslands · forager bands
- **Era:** Stone Age
- **Difficulty:** 1

**Process**

| Stage | Input | Techniques | Output |
|-------|-------|------------|--------|
| 1 | grasses | thresh, winnow | loose grain |
| 2 | loose grain, water | smash, boil, mix | wild grass gruel |

**Historical context:** Before domestication, hunter-gatherers pounded wild cereal heads and boiled the fragments into the oldest known cooked staple.

**Cultural significance:** Gruel is the bridge between foraging and agriculture — proof that grasses could sustain camps through winter.

#### Charred Grain Cakes
- **Origin:** Levant · Natufian culture
- **Era:** Stone Age
- **Difficulty:** 2

**Process**

| Stage | Input | Techniques | Output |
|-------|-------|------------|--------|
| 1 | grasses | thresh, winnow | grain |
