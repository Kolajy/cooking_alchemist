❔ How to Play

Drag ingredients from the Pantry onto the counter, then use the Action Bar to cook and discover new recipes.

🗄️ Pantry

  • Drag an ingredient onto the counter, or tap to place it in the center.
  • Filter by State (Primal, Raw, Prepared) and Type (Produce, Forage, etc.).

🍳 Action Bar

  • Combine — Select it, then drag one ingredient onto another (or use the Combine sub-action).
  • Separate — Pick Separate, then click an ingredient on the counter (highlighted targets are valid).
  • Force — Crush and grind with techniques like Smash and Pound.
  • Heat — Unlocks at 50 recipe discoveries; apply heat to cook and transform ingredients.
  • Time — Unlocks at 200 recipe discoveries; steep, rest, or ferment ingredients.

🎓 Skills & Progress

  • Open the Skills tab in the pantry to see your unlocked actions and exp.
  • Practicing techniques and discovering recipes earns exp and unlocks new actions.
  • Use the Progress Map to explore how ingredients connect.
  • The Recipe Book lists everything you have discovered so far.
  • Open Settings (,) to export or import a save, adjust sound, or reset progress.

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

  • 1–5 Select method (Combine, Separate, Force, Heat, Time)
  • [ ] Previous / next sub-technique
  • Enter Apply active technique to counter
  • U or ⌘Z Undo last counter change
  • C Clear counter
  • / Focus pantry search · P K J Pantry / Skills / Journal
  • B Recipe book · M Progress map · , Settings · S Sound · ? Help
  • Esc Close dialog or leave progress map
